About us

RH Business Solutions & Marketing Management

A recruitment and HR solutions company based in Dubai, with business setup, travel, development and marketing services alongside.

Who we are

We turn ideas into successful businesses

We are a recruitment and HR solutions company based in Dubai. We supply skilled and unskilled workers to companies — one by one or in bulk — from inside the UAE and from African and Asian countries.

Alongside hiring, we help clients set up companies, arrange visit visas and travel, and grow online through development, e-commerce and marketing. Our aim is to give every client modern, optimised services and a partner they can trust.
